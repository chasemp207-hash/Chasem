# 4-Week Content Calendar (Mon Oct 19 to Sun Nov 15, 2026)

**Cadence:** 3 to 4 feed posts/Reels a week, plus 3 or more stories a week. Batch-film on one practice night (about 1 hour) so school weeks stay manageable.
**Account:** IG @chase_m.music
**Ground rules for party footage:**
- Get the chapter's OK to film.
- Ask people before they're clearly on camera, and delete clips if anyone asks you to.
- Don't post visible drinking, anyone who might be underage, or anything that could get the house in trouble.
- Wide shots of the crowd are safer than close-ups of faces.
**Gig dates:** Only post "this Saturday" style announcements for parties that are actually confirmed. Fill in [GIG DATE]/[EVENT] as they come up, and swap in a practice clip when there's no gig.
