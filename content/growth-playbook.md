# Growth Playbook (one page)

## 1. Filming good DJ clips on a phone
- **Vertical (9:16), 1080p or 4K at 30/60fps.** Wipe the lens first.
- **Stabilize:** use a cheap tripod or gooseneck clamp, and shoot from a 45° angle above the decks so your hands, the jog wheels and your face are all in frame.
- **Light:** at home, use a desk lamp plus one colored LED. At gigs, put the party lights behind you, not behind the phone.
- **Audio:** phone mics distort at parties. For mix clips, record from the controller's master/booth out (USB audio interface or a phone adapter), or record the set in your DJ software and sync it in CapCut. Use the phone audio only for crowd noise.
- **Shot list per gig (5 minutes total):** setup timelapse, the first drop, a wide crowd shot from behind the decks, a hands close-up during a transition, and the last song.

## 2. Hook in the first 2 seconds
- Start mid-action: the drop, the crowd reaction or your hands on the fader. Never open with a blank intro.
- Add on-screen text right away: "Wait for this transition", "POV: DJing my brother's frat", "Would you mix these?"
- Cut so the payoff lands by second 5–7. Keep transition clips to 10–20s and recaps to 30s or less.

## 3. Music copyright on IG and TikTok (keep it simple)
- Your audio contains other people's songs, so **any clip can be muted or taken down.** Short clips (under 30–60s) get flagged less often, but there's no guarantee.
- **Account type matters:** IG/TikTok business accounts get a limited "commercial" music library. Creator/personal accounts have more licensed music. A creator account is usually the better fit for a DJ.
- Don't upload hour-long mixes to IG or TikTok. Host full mixes on **Mixcloud** (which licenses DJ mixes in many countries) or SoundCloud (which can take down mixes), and link them in your bio.
- If a clip gets muted, re-cut it shorter or pair it with a platform-library version of the track.
- This isn't legal advice. Check each platform's current music rules.

## 4. Getting the frat and partygoers to tag and share
- **Ask before you film** and tell people "I'll post this, tag yourself." People share clips they look good in.
- **Post recaps within 24 hours** while the party is still being talked about, and tag the host account [FRAT HANDLE] plus your brother.
- Use **IG Collab posts** with the host chapter or student org so the post shows up on both feeds.
- Put a story slide with your @ on the screen/booth, or a small sign that says "Tag @@Paulmchase".
- Repost every story you're tagged in (it's free content and builds goodwill). Send the host a clip pack after each gig.
- Never post visible drinking, anyone who might be underage, or anything the chapter didn't agree to.

## 5. Posting times (starting points, then use your Insights)
- **Weeknights 7–10pm ET**, when students are done with class or practice. **Thu/Fri 5–8pm** for weekend announcements. **Sun 11am–2pm** for recaps.
- After 3 weeks, check IG Insights and TikTok Analytics under "Most active times" and adjust.

## 6. Weekly metrics checklist (10 minutes every Sunday)
- [ ] Followers on IG and TikTok (write down the change this week)
- [ ] Top post by views, and its hook (what worked?)
- [ ] Average watch time / % who watched the full clip on the top 2 videos
- [ ] Shares + saves (these matter more than likes)
- [ ] Profile visits and link-in-bio clicks
- [ ] Booking DMs/inquiries received → booked → $ earned
- [ ] Collabs pitched / confirmed (student orgs, chapters)
- [ ] Next week: 1 thing to repeat, 1 thing to drop
