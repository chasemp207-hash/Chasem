# 90-Day Roadmap: Oct 19, 2026 to Jan 31, 2027

**Goals:** first paid gig, first gig outside Babson, a mix + IG presence, 3 repeat clients, and a realistic revenue target.

**Revenue target (estimate):** **$1,000-$1,800 gross** across the 15 weeks, from about **6-9 paid gigs** at $125-$250 (mostly sound provided). Stretch: $2,500 if a formal or two with your PA lands in late January. December and early January will be slow. Treat that as build time.

## College calendar anchors
- **Late Oct:** Halloween weekend (Oct 30-31, 2026 is Fri-Sat), the busiest party weekend of the fall
- **Early-mid Nov:** Fall formals and date parties wrap up
- **Late Nov:** Thanksgiving break, low demand
- **Early-mid Dec:** Finals, near-zero demand. Holiday org parties happen right before
- **Mid Dec to mid Jan:** Winter break, students away. Possible home-town or holiday gigs
- **Late Jan:** Spring semester starts, **rush/recruitment** events, and social chairs plan the **spring formal season** (Feb-Apr). Book now.

## Repeat-client playbook
- After every gig: thank-you text within 24 hrs, ask for a testimonial and the next date.
- Keep a list of every social chair/organizer. Positions turn over in January, so ask who's next.
- Offer repeat clients a locked rate for the semester.
