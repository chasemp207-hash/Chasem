# Chasem DJ: Rate Card (Beginner to Intermediate, Boston Area)

*Drafted 2026-10-19. Market figures are cited below. Suggested rates are **estimates** based on those benchmarks, not on hard Boston averages, because none were published.*

## What the market looks like

| Benchmark | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Boston, MA DJ listings | ~$50-$150/hr; flat events ~$200-$650 (asking prices) | [The Bash, Boston MA](https://www.thebash.com:443/search/dj-boston-ma) |
| Boston wedding DJ package (pro tier) | $1,700 for 5 hrs incl. 2 PA speakers | [Zola / Disc Jockey Boston](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-bands-djs/disc-jockey-boston) |
| Student-run DJ services (other schools) | $60-$115/hr, or $125-$200 flat for 4-5 hrs | [WKNC](https://wknc.org/about/dj-services), [Kent State Student Media](https://kentstatestudentmedia.com/advertising/mobile-dj-service), [UCF Knights of the Turntables](https://osicms.smca.ucf.edu/agencies/knights-of-the-turntables) |

**Takeaway:** student DJs price around $50-$125/hr. Established Boston pros charge several times that. You start at the low-middle of the student range and work up.

## Rate card (starting rates, Fall 2026)

Rates include 3 hours of playing time, setup/teardown, and planning a playlist with the host.

| Event type | Venue/client provides sound | You bring your own PA | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frat/sorority party (30-100 ppl) | $150 flat | $250 flat | Bread and butter. Fast to book, repeatable. |
| Date party / formal (venue, 50-150 ppl) | $250 flat | $375-$450 flat | Usually booked by a social chair weeks ahead. Charge more because it needs a mic, clean edits and a set timeline. |
| Student org event (club, cultural, Greek philanthropy) | $150-$200 flat | $250-$300 flat | Orgs often have a budget line and need an invoice/W-9. Ask early. |
| House party (private, off-campus) | $125 flat | $200 flat | Highest shutdown risk. Take the full deposit up front. |
| Small bar/lounge night | $100-$200 per night | n/a (bar has sound) | Often a door split or flat rate. Take it for exposure only if the slot builds your name (see below). |

**Hourly equivalent:** about **$50-$85/hr** (sound provided) or **$75-$125/hr** (your PA). Quote **flat rates** for parties because they're easier to sell. Use **hourly** only for open-ended bar nights or events without a set end time.

**Minimum booking:** 2 hours.

## Deposit and payment

- **50% non-refundable deposit** holds the date. No deposit means the date isn't booked.
- **Balance due before the first song** of the event (Venmo/Zelle), not "after the party".
- Student orgs paying by check or invoice: get the paperwork started at booking, because school accounts can be slow.
- Use the booking agreement (`booking-agreement.md`) for **every** paid gig, even for friends.

## Raising rates as demand grows

Raise prices when **any** of these happen:
1. You turn down 2+ gigs in a month because you're already booked.
2. You've done 5+ paid gigs and have a mix plus clips on IG to show.
3. You buy gear that changes what you deliver (a sub, lights).

**How:** raise by **$25-$50 per tier** each step (about 15-20%). Lock in loyal repeat clients at their old rate for one semester, then move them up. Natural moments to reprice: **January 2027** (spring rush and formal season) and next **fall**.

Rough target trajectory (estimate):
- Fall 2026: $150-$250 per party
- Spring 2027: $200-$350 per party, $450-$600 per formal with your PA
- After 1 year + strong referrals: $75-$125/hr range, consistent with the top of student services and the low end of Boston listings

## "Can you do it for free / for exposure?"

Default answer: **"I've got a student rate, and it's $X. For your org I can do [smaller package] for $Y."** Always offer a smaller paid option instead of a flat no.

Free is fine **only** when you choose it, and only if at least one of these is true:
- It's a **showcase** in front of people who book DJs (a social chair council, the IFC/Panhel, a bar manager), *and* you get something concrete: a recording, a tag on IG, a promised paid follow-up date.
- It's a **charity or philanthropy** event you actually care about.
- It's your brother's frat. Even then, start charging a "friends rate" (e.g. $100) now so it's normal.

Cap free gigs at **one per month**. Put "exposure" deals in writing too: "No fee. In exchange: tag @[handle], allow filming, and refer me to [X]."

Script: *"Thanks for thinking of me! I'm booking paid gigs now, and my party rate starts at $150. If the budget's tight, I can do a 2-hour set with your sound for $100."*
