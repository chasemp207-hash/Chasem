## 2. Fee and payment
- **Total fee:** $[AMOUNT] for [HOURS] hours of music, plus add-ons: [LIST OR "NONE"]
- **Deposit:** $[50% OF FEE], due by [DATE] to hold the date. **Non-refundable** except as stated in Section 3.
- **Balance:** $[AMOUNT], due **before music starts** on the event date.
- **Payment method:** Venmo @[HANDLE] / Zelle [PHONE OR EMAIL] / [CHECK OR INVOICE]
- **Overtime:** Extra time only if the DJ agrees on the night, at $[RATE] per hour (billed in [30/60]-minute blocks), paid that night.

## 3. Cancellation
- **Client cancels 14+ days before:** DJ keeps the deposit. Nothing more is owed.
- **Client cancels less than 14 days before:** DJ keeps the deposit, and [25% / 50%] of the balance is owed.
- **DJ cancels:** DJ refunds 100% of payments received and will try to recommend a replacement DJ.
- **Weather/emergency/school-ordered cancellation:** Deposit may be applied to a new date within [90] days.

## 4. Equipment and damage
- **Sound provided by:** [DJ / CLIENT / VENUE]. If the venue provides it, Client confirms it works and someone who knows it is on site at setup.
- DJ's equipment is handled **only by the DJ**. Guests stay out of the DJ area.
- Client is responsible for **damage to DJ equipment caused by guests** (spilled drinks, falls, theft), up to replacement cost, payable within [14] days.
- Client provides: a sturdy table ([6 ft]), a grounded outlet within [25 ft], and cover/shelter if outdoors.
- DJ may stop playing and protect the gear if conditions are unsafe (rain, crowd on the table, fights). This does not create a refund.

## 5. Noise complaints, police, and shutdowns
- Client is responsible for permits, noise rules, venue rules, and guest conduct, **including alcohol and underage drinking**.
- If police, campus security, a landlord, or the venue end or quiet the event, **the full fee is still owed** and no refund is due.
- DJ will turn the volume down when asked by police, security, or venue staff.

## 6. Music
- Client may send a must-play list of up to [NUMBER] songs by [DATE].
- **Do-not-play list:** [SONGS / ARTISTS / GENRES]
- Clean edits only? [YES / NO]
- DJ makes final calls on song order and on guest requests.
- Music licensing (if the event is public or ticketed) is the venue/Client's responsibility. [VERIFY FOR EACH VENUE]

- Food/drink for the DJ: [PROVIDED / NOT PROVIDED]. Parking: [DETAILS]
- The DJ is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client or venue.
